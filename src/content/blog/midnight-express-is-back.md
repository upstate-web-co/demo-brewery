It's been fourteen months since the last batch of Midnight Express went into bourbon barrels. That was November 2024 — a cold Tuesday, a full day of brewing, and then the hardest part: waiting.

## What's in the bottle

Midnight Express is an imperial stout — 10.2% ABV, brewed with midnight wheat, chocolate malt, and a healthy dose of flaked oats for body. After primary fermentation, it goes into freshly emptied bourbon barrels from a distillery we're not allowed to name (their rules, not ours).

Fourteen months later, you get this: dark as engine oil, thick enough to coat a glass, with layers of vanilla, charred oak, dark chocolate, and a warmth that builds from the first sip.
